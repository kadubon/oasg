source bytes, independently reconstructed measurements and scoped host signatures.

lifecycle select a bounded routing improvement; native OAWM verifies, promotes and
qualifies its procedural record. A fresh process retrieves it for later eligible
CCR work. Native VEK checks and CAIT-through-CCR reconciliation preserve their
separate authority boundaries.

Add exact-version/content companion checks, negative controls, stale-state and
crash tests, immutable reconciliation snapshots, withdrawal and policy rollback.
Legacy dependencies, canonical signing domains and schemas are unchanged.
No external model experiment, semantic performance claim or automatic reward is added.

Add protected OIDC release automation and external wheel/sdist qualification.
Publication status is established by successful workflow and public-install evidence,
not by this changelog entry.
